Course Pre-requisites

Academic:
Completion of:
  • Year 12 of High School with an ATAR of 60 or equivalent; or
  • A currently accredited Tertiary Preparation Program, NSW TAFE Tertiary Preparation Certificate (TPC); or a Foundation Year Course; or
  • A Vocational Education & Training (VET) Qualification from an accredited Australian VET Provider (RTO): Certificate IV (AQF 4); or Diploma (AQF 5), or an Advanced Diploma (AQF6); or
  • An Australian Higher Education qualification from a registered higher education provider: Diploma (AQF5); or Associate Degree (AQF6); or a Bachelors' degree (AQF7)

English Language Proficiency (ELP):
If English is not the applicant's principal language or the applicant has not completed a secondary or tertiary education qualification in English in last two years, the applicant must also satisfy the following requirement of English language proficiency:
  • IELTS Test: 6.0 with no band lower than 5.5.
  • TOEFL (internet-based) 70 (minimum of 20 in writing, 16 in speaking, 11 in listening and reading).
  • Pearson Test of English (PTE) Academic: Overall score of 50 (minimum of 42 in listening, reading, speaking and writing)
  • English for Academic Purposes (EAP) course completed at an accredited Australian ELICOS provider.

APPENDIX A: GLOSSARY OF TERMS (OFFER OF ADMISSION)
AQF Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF). The AQF classifies qualifications issued by registered education providers across 10 levels. A bachelor's degree is at AQF 7; and a Graduate Diploma is at AQF 8.
eCoE Electronic Confirmation of Enrolment (eCoE). This is a one-page document issued by a CRICOS-registered provider on behalf of the Australian government that includes the course name, Start and End Date, and other information. A CoE is required to apply to the Department of Home Affairs for a student visa or to extend a current student visa.
Compassionate and Compelling Circumstances Circumstances that include the death of or serious illness or injury to the student or their immediate family members (parents, grandparents, siblings); the student's involvement in or witnessing of a serious accident or violent crime; or a major political upheaval, pandemic, or natural disaster that occurred in Australia or the student's home country or region.
Extraordinary Circumstances When a situation occurs that is beyond the control of the student and the consequences of which could not have been avoided, even if all reasonable measures had been taken.
Provider Default When an education provider fails to provide a course to an enrolled student on an agreed start date; or when the education provider stops providing a course to a student at a location any time after the course has commenced, but before it has been completed.
Taught-out and Teach Out Plan In the event CIHE discontinues a course, in most cases it will be "taught-out" (taught until all enrolled students complete the course within the minimum course completion time). Currently enrolled students will be issued a teach-out plan that outlines the requirements to complete their course within the minimal course completion time; and any changes to the course's professional accreditation or recognition, if applicable.
Third-party Provider A third-party provider means another company/external organization with whom CIHE has an agreement to deliver or administer a component of the course. Education Agents are currently third-party providers. CIHE quality assures all third-party arrangements per applicable CIHE policies.
